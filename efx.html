Common Instrument EFX

ERRError Randomize pitch and params (not Hypersynth)
SMXModFX Send ModFX amount
SDLDelay Send Delay amount
SRVReverb Send Reverb amount
FILFilter Type This instrument's filter (global is DJ*)

RESResonance Filter peak
AMPAmp Gain into the limiter; LIM sets how it clips

PANPan 00 (left) – 80 (center) – FF (right)
Sequence Flow
HOPHop Phrase: Y=row of next phrase (FF stops). Table: Y=row, X=times
KIL/OFFKill / Note Off XX ticks: KIL cuts, OFF starts ADSR release
INSInstrument XX: switch to instrument XX
NXTNext Track XX: instrument (00–7F) on track to the right
RETRetrigger X: volume (0–7 down, 8–F up), Y: ticks
REP/RTORepeat / Ramp Steps the previous FX (80–FF down); RTO stops it
CHAChance Phrase: X=left, Y=right (0–F). Table: XX (00–FF)
NTHNth Trigger Skip left (X) or right (Y) side by loop count
RNDRandom Randomize previous FX; X/Y: digit range (0–F)
RNLRandom Left Randomize the FX to its left; in FX1: note and instrument
RMXRemix X selects tracks to the left; Y sets phrase row
MTTMicro Timing Sub-tick shift (1/8 tick); 80–FF = earlier
