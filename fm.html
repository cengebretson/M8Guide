How FM Works
Carriers & ModulatorsRATIO · LEV
  • Carriers are heard; modulators reshape a carrier's tone
  • Modulator LEV sets brightness; RATIO multiplies the note
  • Whole-number ratios sound harmonic, odd ones metallic
Op-Mods (the MOD rows)MOD1–4
  • MODS page: set a slot's DEST to MOD1–4 to feed that op-mod
  • An operator's MOD slot applies it to LEV, RAT, PIT or FBK
  • One envelope can drive several operators; FM1–FM4 automate
Operator Controls

MOD RATRatio slot Adds 0.00 to +16.00 to the ratio
MOD PITPitch slot Adds semitones to that operator

Useful Algorithms
00A → B → C → D Serial chain: bright, complex stacks
04A + B + C → D Three modulators into one carrier
08A → B + C + D One modulator shared by three carriers
0BA + B + C + D Additive: four carriers, no FM

Drums & Percussion
FM KickMOD RAT
  • A 2-op stack with the modulator at LEV 00: one SIN carrier
  • MOD1 → AHD, short DEC → op-mod on the carrier's RAT slot
  • MOD2 → AHD on volume; its DEC sets the kick's length
SnareNOISE · AMP
  • A tone carrier plus a NOISE operator for the rattle
  • Short envelope on carrier LEV through an op-mod
  • Raise AMP (around 20) so clipping makes a stick transient
BellODD RATIO
  • Modulator RATIO off the grid (e.g. 3.50), low feedback
  • One AHD op-mod on both modulator LEV and carrier LEV
  • Brightness fades with the volume, like a struck bell
Hi-HatsNOISE
  • NOISE shape on the carrier, very short AHD on volume
  • High-pass FILTER; KIL 02 for closed, longer DEC for open
TomsMOD RAT
  • Start from the FM kick with a higher note and slower RAT decay
  • Step through a few notes for a tom fill
Layer Two SoundsTWO STACKS
  • Pick an algorithm with two separate 2-op stacks
  • One stack for the body, the other for a click or noise tail
Basses
Round FM BassRATIO 0.50
  • Modulator RATIO 0.50 or 0.25 adds sub-like weight
  • Short AHD on the modulator's LEV slot for the pluck
  • Keep DEL and REV sends low so the low end stays tight
Growl & WobbleFB · FBK
  • Raise the modulator's FB for grit
  • LFO → op-mod → the modulator's FBK slot to wobble it
  • LIM SIN or POST:AD thickens without harsh clipping
No More ClicksDE1 · ATK
  • Long decays clash with fast notes: shorten them with DE1
  • Still clicking? Raise the volume envelope's ATK above 00
Chords, Pads & Leads
Four-Voice ChordsALGO 0B
  • ALGO 0B makes all four operators carriers
  • RATIOs 1.00 · 1.26 · 1.50 · 2.00 = major chord plus octave
  • Minor: 1.00 · 1.19 · 1.50; balance voices with LEV
Dub PadALGO 08
  • One modulator shared by three carriers
  • Carriers on a minor chord across octaves (1.00 · 2.38 · 5.99)
  • Detune the modulator slightly; slow AHD, MFX and REV sends
PWM LeadIMP · FBK
  • Carrier shape IMP; its FB sets the pulse width
  • LFO → op-mod → the carrier's FBK slot sweeps the width
Wavetable ScanW SHAPES
  • Pick a W shape; FB moves through the wavetable
  • A slow LFO on the FBK slot evolves the tone over bars
Expressive BrightnessTRACKING
  • MOD → TRACKING, SRC VELOCITY → op-mod on modulator LEV
  • Soft notes are mellow, hard notes bite, like an electric piano
Detuned Unison PadALGO 0B
  • ALGO 0B with RATIOs 1.00 · 1.01 · 0.99 · 2.00
  • SAW or SQU shapes, slow ADSR, and an MFX send for chorus
Additive OrganALGO 0B
  • ALGO 0B with RATIOs 1.00 · 2.00 · 3.00 · 4.00 on SIN shapes
  • Each operator's LEV works like a drawbar
VibratoLFO · PVB
  • MOD → LFO on pitch with a small AMT; FREQ sets the speed
  • Or PVB in the phrase for vibrato on single notes
Per-Step AccentsFM1 · DE1
  • FM1 on a step changes op-mod 1's amount for that note
  • DE1 lengthens or shortens envelope 1's decay per step
