Good to Know
8-Bit Character256 LEVELS
  • Wavsynth uses 256 levels, so saws look like staircases
  • Those edges add bright, small-speaker harmonics
  • Pulse waves have only two levels and stay clean
Same Tables in FMFM W SHAPES
  • FM Synth operators can use these wavetables too
  • There, FB scans the table instead of adding feedback
Wavsynth Recipes
Chip Pulse BassPULSE 50%
  • SHAPE PULSE 50%: only two levels, so 8-bit costs nothing
  • Short AHD on volume; WARP to change its hollow tone
  • A little MFX send (chorus) widens it
PWM LeadSCAN
  • SHAPE PULSE 50%, then an LFO on SCAN sweeps the width
  • Larger SIZE makes the sweep smoother
Chip ChordsARP 47
  • PULSE 25% with ARP 47 (major) or ARP 37 (minor)
  • ARC sets the arpeggio mode and speed in ticks
  • PVB after the attack for a classic wobbly lead
Noise DrumsNOISE
  • SHAPE NOISE with a very short AHD for hats and snares
  • PITCHED NOISE plays in tune: the note sets its color
  • Fast PBN down on pitched noise for laser zaps
Wavetable MorphSCN
  • Pick a wavetable (e.g. 26 HRM:HARMONIC or 3F EFX:VOCAL)
  • An LFO or table steps on SCAN morph through its 64 waves
Sync-Style SweepsMULT
  • AHD on MULT stacks repeats of the shape as it rises
  • Sounds like a hard-sync sweep on leads and basses
Tame or CrunchWAV LP · CUTOFF
  • 8-bit saws step like a staircase and add bright edges
  • A normal low-pass tames them; WAV filters add crunch
Duty-Cycle SwitchingOSC 00–03
  • OSC on a step switches between the four pulse widths
  • 12% is thin and nasal, 50% is hollow and round
Chip KickSINE · AHD PITCH
  • SHAPE SINE with a short AHD on volume
  • A fast AHD on pitch drops it for the thump
Echo ChannelDEL · V
  • Copy the lead phrase to a free track with lower V values
  • DEL on its first row delays it for a classic tracker echo
Hypersynth Parameters
SHAPE12 shapes (6.6) Saw variants, soft square, triangle, sine variants
CHORD16 banks, up to 6 intervals SHIFT + OPT copies; SHIFT + EDIT pastes
SCALESong scale for the chord 00 follows the song or track scale
SHIFTCrossfade Between intervals 1–3 and 4–6
SWARMDetune Spreads each note's oscillator pair
WIDTHStereo spread 00 mono to FF full stereo
SUBOSCSquare sub Below 80: two octaves down · above 80: one
FXCRD · SWM · WID · SUB Change chords and tone per step
Hypersynth Recipes
Lush PadSWARM · WIDTH
  • CHORD: major 7th = 0, 4, 7, 11 semitones (decimal)
  • Moderate SWARM, WIDTH toward FF, MFX and REV sends
  • ADSR on volume, slow ATK and long REL; end notes with OFF
Chord ProgressionsCRD
  • Build a bank for each chord shape you need
  • CRD on a step switches banks; the note sets the root
Evolving ExtensionsSHIFT
  • Intervals 1–3: a triad · 4–6: its 7th, 9th and 11th
  • A slow LFO on SHIFT morphs from plain to rich
Supersaw StabSWARM · AHD
  • SHAPE SAW, high SWARM, WIDTH FF; short volume AHD
  • A DEL send for trance-style echoes
Bass + Chord in OneSUBOSC
  • Raise SUBOSC for the root under the chord
  • Low CUTOFF and a short AHD on it for a stab
Stay in KeySCALE
  • Set SCALE to a song scale so intervals follow the key
  • SCA or SCG changes the scale mid-song
Solid Low EndWIDTH
  • Keep WIDTH low when SUBOSC or low notes carry the bass
  • Save full width for pads that sit above it
Pluck ChordsCUTOFF · AHD
  • Low SWARM, short AHD on CUTOFF with some RES
  • Play off-beat chords for house and garage stabs
