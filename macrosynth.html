Basics
Two Knobs, 44 ModelsSHAPE
  • SHAPE picks the model; TIMBRE and COLOR vary per model
  • OSC switches models per step; TBR and COL move the knobs
  • Put an LFO or AHD on TIMBRE or COLOR for movement
Lo-Fi CrunchDEGRADE · REDUX
  • DEGRADE lowers the sample rate, REDUX the bit depth
  • DEG and RED in a phrase crunch single notes or fills

Supersaw Lead0E SAW SWARM
  • Raise TIMBRE until the seven saws beat and spread
  • COLOR high-passes the lows so it sits above the bass
  • ADSR on volume with a long REL, plus a REV send
Sync Sweep08 SAW SYNC
  • AHD or LFO on TIMBRE sweeps the synced harmonics
  • COLOR blends in the plain oscillator to soften it
Sub Bass05 SQUARE SUB
  • COLOR sets the sub one or two octaves down
  • Low CUTOFF and a short AHD on volume keep it tight

Talking Lead16 VOWEL
  • A slow LFO on TIMBRE morphs through a, e, i, o, u
  • COLOR with a lower note shifts the voice deeper or older
CZ Zap Bass11 DIGITAL FILTER LP
  • Short AHD on TIMBRE sweeps the built-in cutoff down

Plucked Lead1C PLUCKED
  • TIMBRE sets how long the string rings
  • Very low notes become knocks and wood percussion
  • A DEL send and PSL slides give a guitar-like line
Bells & Mallets20 STRUCK BELL
  • Low COLOR is sweet and tuned, high COLOR is clangy
  • TIMBRE shortens the ring for marimba-like hits
Drone Strings1D BOWED
  • Hold notes with an ADSR on volume; end them with OFF
  • A slow LFO on COLOR moves the bow for a living tone
