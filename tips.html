Drums

DRUM ENVPunchy Transient Drum envelope on pitch: high peak, fast drop, short body
KIL 02Tight Hats Hard-cut closed hats after 2 ticks for a crisp tick
RET 03Hat & Snare Rolls Retrig every 3 ticks; first digit 1–7 fades, 9–F swells
NXTLayered Hits Fire a second instrument on the next track (clap on snare)
OSC 21Metal Percussion STRUCK DRUM / BELL, or PLUCKED on very low notes
ERRLo-Fi Drift A little ERR jitters pitch and params on each hit
VELAccents Vary hat velocity so a straight grid stops sounding robotic
CUTOpening Hats Ramp cutoff across a bar so hats brighten into the drop
PANWide Percussion Alternate PAN on shakers; keep kick and snare centered
PITTuned Drums Tune the kick and toms to the song's key
Rhythm & Groove
GRVSwing Groove 07 05 or 08 04 swings 16ths (06 06 is straight)
CHA 8FGhost Notes Note plays about half the time; right-side FX always run
DEL 03Late Hit Push one hit half a step (6 ticks = 1 step)
MTTHumanize Sub-tick nudge in 1/8 ticks; 80–FF pulls early
00Odd Meters A 00 value in a groove skips that step entirely
GRVPolyrhythm Give tracks grooves of different lengths to drift in phase
NTHFills Play a hit only on certain loops; see on-screen help
RNDPer-Hit Variation Randomize the previous FX within a digit range
SEDRepeatable Random Set a seed at a section start so CHA and RND repeat
GRVTriplets A groove of 04 makes that track's steps 16th-note triplets
GRVHalf-Time A groove of 0C doubles each step's length
RMXCut-Up Jumps Moves the playhead on all tracks to the left

Sampling

EDIT ×2Resample Render a selection to a sampler instrument, then mangle it

Table Tricks
TIC 01Flams & Grace Notes Softer note a semitone below, one tick before the hit
HOP 00Looping Arp Intervals in the N column, HOP 00 at the end repeats them
HOP 3XLoop N Times First digit counts repeats before moving on
V COLCustom Envelope Step the volume column for gates and tremolo
TIC 00Per-Note Automation Advance a row per note: cycle cutoff or pan each hit
TIC FEKit on One Track Note picks the row; INS per row swaps instruments
TIC FDVelocity Layers Velocity picks the row, so harder hits change the sound
THOTable Sections Split one table into parts and hop between them
TBLSwap Tables Change an instrument's table mid-phrase for variations
Mix & FX

SDL FFDub Throw Full delay send on one hit, back to 00 on the next step

DJCBuild-Up Sweep the global DJ filter over 4–8 bars; DJR adds bite

SMXWidth A touch of ModFX send widens pads and leads
USBMultitrack Recording Multichannel USB audio sends each track to a DAW
